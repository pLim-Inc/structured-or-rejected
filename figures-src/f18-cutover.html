Ten rules
1Sequence by dependency, not by deadline.

Data, then format, then volume.

6Freeze what the test proved.

No mass change to payees, no transport on the format.

2One change per run.

Not the format, the channel and the workflow together.

7Empty the pipe before the switch.

No open proposal, no batch waiting in the old format.

3Go live by bank and payment method.

Bank A in March, bank B in April.

8Rehearse the way back, and time it.

A named owner and a written trigger.

4The first production run is a pilot.

Nothing else leaves until status and statement agree.

9Two people check endpoints and certificates.

No certificate expires within 90 days.

5Stay away from month-end.

Three working days clear of a period close. No payroll or tax.

10No data is a no.

A criterion without a measurement fails.
