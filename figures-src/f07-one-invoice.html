What the example shows
1

A missing town cost one day and one rejected payment. Fixed at control point 1, it would have cost nothing.

2

The fix belongs to master data, not to treasury. The reason code has to reach the right team.

3

The bank's booking date ends up on the supplier's invoice. Payments and invoicing are one loop.
