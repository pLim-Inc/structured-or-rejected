Three ways to connect
  • Standard practiceFile interface. Download, upload to the bank portal, upload the statement. No status comes back.
  • RecommendedSAP Multi-Bank Connectivity. SAP runs the connection. Status reports and statements return on their own.
  • Private cloud, on-premiseYour own channel. Swift, EBICS or host-to-host through your middleware.
